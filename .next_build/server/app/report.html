Statutory Notice & Independence Disclaimer

ClaimKavach is an independent, open-source verification tool and is not affiliated with the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA), the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI), the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, or any private hospital or insurance company.

All findings produced by ClaimKavach are mathematical computations and statutory comparisons against published regulatory notifications (such as NPPA DPCO 2013 ceiling orders and IRDAI Master Circulars). This tool is designed solely for consumer awareness, pre-flight review, and consumer grievance preparation. It does not constitute legal, medical, or financial advice.

Data privacy: 100% in-memory processing. Closing this browser tab or clicking “Clear Data” permanently purges all uploaded documents and patient data.
