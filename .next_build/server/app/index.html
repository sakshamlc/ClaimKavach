Honest Verification Scope

Zero invented rules. Every finding cites an official gazetted instrument. Un-sourced categories remain honestly unrepresented.

What We Check Today

  • NPPA DPCO Drug Ceilings: Scheduled formulation statutory caps from S.O. 1582(E) and retail formulation caps from S.O. 5975(E).
  • Coronary Stent Price Caps: Bare Metal Stents and Drug Eluting Stents statutory ceilings under S.O. 1587(E).
  • Deterministic Arithmetic: Line item rate × quantity integrity and grand total sum reconciliation.
  • Printed MRP Safeguard: Flags instances where billed rate exceeds printed MRP on bill items.

What We Don't Yet Check (Pending Sources)

  • Knee Implants Price Tables: Extension S.O. 6222(E) is tracked, but root price tables from S.O. 5190(E) / S.O. 2668(E) are pending drop-in. Items are marked NOT_CHECKED.
  • IRDAI Annexure-I Non-Payables: Full schedule tables are pending manual drop-in into docs/sources/raw/.
  • Room Tariff Bundling Schedules: Un-sourced clinical bundling rules remain zeroed rather than guessed.
  • Subjective Medical Necessity: ClaimKavach never judges clinical appropriateness or doctor prescriptions.
