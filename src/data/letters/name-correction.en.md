# Sample Request Letter: Correction of Name in Bank Records

**Date:** {{date}}  
**To:** The Branch Manager, {{bankName}}, {{branch}}  

**Subject:** Request for correction of Account Holder Name as per Aadhaar Card (A/C No. ending in ...{{accountLast4}})  

Respected Sir / Madam,  

I maintain a savings bank account with your branch bearing account number ending in **...{{accountLast4}}**. Presently, my name is recorded in the bank passbook as **{{currentValue}}**.  

However, as per my official Aadhaar card, my correct legal name is **{{correctValue}}**. Due to this difference in name spelling and word order, my government scheme benefits and Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) are facing rejections.  

I kindly request you to update my name in your core banking records to match my Aadhaar card (**{{correctValue}}**) and issue an updated passbook.  

**Enclosures:**  
1. Self-attested photocopy of Aadhaar Card  
2. Photocopy of Bank Passbook  

Thanking you,  

Yours faithfully,  
**({{correctValue}})**  
Signature of Account Holder
